# House Theory

House Theory is a single-page estate agency website for a design-led agency that sells thoughtful homes in urban and rural locations across the UK. The page presents featured sales, past sales, press quotes, a seller story, and a newsletter signup.

## What the page includes

- **Navigation** — Buy, Sell, Journey, Directory, Shop, About, and Subscribe, with the House Theory name in the centre.
- **Editorial hero** — A featured story, “Space Age: six space-saving London home,” dated 19 March 2022.
- **Press quotes** — Short quotes attributed to the Evening Standard and GQ.
- **Featured sales** — Six current listings with name, price, location, and tenure (freehold, leasehold, or share of freehold).
- **Seller story** — A testimonial from Brandon Griffin.
- **About** — A short statement on how House Theory works with buyers and sellers.
- **Featured past sales** — Sold homes in Notting Hill and Sturmer Way II.
- **Footer** — Company links, contact details, and a newsletter form with a privacy checkbox.

## Project files

- `favicon.ico` — browser tab icon

## View the site

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder locally:

```bash
npx --yes serve .
```

Then visit the local address printed in the terminal. The layout is written for desktop and smaller screens.
